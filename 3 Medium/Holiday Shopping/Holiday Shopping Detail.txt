Hint 1/7
Because F is the only person sitting in a chair, S must be in R3 in one of Santa's Lodge squares (not the chair). Cross out all other squares in R3 and the chair in Santa's Lodge, since S was alone.

Hint 2/7
Now F's position. Since E is in the Coffee Shop and NOT with F, the only chairs F could be in are the Walkway or Santa's Village. But the two Walkway chairs are on C5, which I occupies, so F has to be the chair at R4 C6. Cross out all other chairs in the puzzle.

Hint 3/7
Now G and J. G is in the Bookstore and J is one row above her, so they need two adjacent rows. Only R1 and R2 work – if G were in the Bookstore in R5, there'd be no room for J above her. So G is R2 C11 and J is somewhere in R1.

Hint 4/7
This narrows D to two squares on C3, beside the register in the Toy Store – cross out the rest of that column. It also leaves B one square in the Walkway in front of a door, R8 C8. S is now narrowed to R3 C7.

Hint 5/7
The only square left for C to be beside a teddy bear is R9 C2, leaving D at R11 C3. E takes the last Coffee Shop square at R7 C10. With one square left in R10 and I on C5, I is at R10 C5.

Hint 6/7
R5 C1 must be occupied as the last square of its column – cross out the rest of that row. R6 C4 is the last square of its row – cross out the rest of that column. This narrows J to R1 C9. Since A is north of V, A is at R5 C1.

Hint 7/7
This leaves the last square R6 C4 for V – hence F is the murderer.
